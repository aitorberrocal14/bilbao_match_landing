Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

In short: this website has no forms, no accounts and no analytics. It does not ask you for anything and it does not record who you are. Personal data only comes into play when you choose to leave the site — to write to us, to subscribe to the newsletter or to register for the event — and this page explains what happens in each case.

2. How we obtain your data

Not through this website. These pages are static. There is no contact form, no subscription form, no login and no analytics or audience measurement of any kind. Nothing is stored in your browser and nothing is sent to us as you read.

Data reaches us in three ways, all of which begin with a deliberate act on your part: writing to us, subscribing to the newsletter on the provider's own form, or registering on the event platform.

As with any website, the server that delivers these pages keeps technical access logs — IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system — for the security and correct operation of the site. Those logs are not used to build any profile or to identify visitors.

3. What we use your data for

  • Answering enquiries. To handle and reply to the questions you send us about the event, your participation, the programme or the platform.
  • Managing the newsletter. To manage the subscription and send information about activities, programmes, services, events and initiatives of the destination.
  • Organising the event. To manage registration, accreditation and participation in Match Bilbao Bizkaia: matching participants, scheduling meetings and running the programme.
  • Publishing the exhibitor directory. To show the participating companies of the destination on this website, so that international buyers can find them.

If data is ever to be used for a different purpose, the person concerned will be informed beforehand.

4. Legal basis

  • Performance of a task carried out in the public interest (Art. 6.1.e GDPR), for handling enquiries and for running the services, programmes and activities of Bilbao Ekintza, including this event.
  • Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR), for subscribing to the newsletter and receiving informational communications.
  • Pre-contractual measures (Art. 6.1.b GDPR), where the request relates to taking part in a programme or service.

Consent may be withdrawn at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

5. How long we keep it

Personal data is kept for as long as is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. It may then be held blocked for the periods established by law, in order to meet legal obligations and to address any liability that may arise.

Newsletter data is kept until the subscriber asks to be removed. Exhibitor details are published in the directory for the edition of the event they registered for.

6. Who receives your data

As a general rule, data is not disclosed to third parties, except where required by law. Providers that supply services to Bilbao Ekintza — technology services, web hosting and management tools — may have access to personal data acting as processors, under the contract required by Article 28 GDPR.

For this event in particular, those providers are the operator of the event platform and the newsletter sending service.

Exhibitors: if you register as an exhibitor, the business details of your company — company name, category, logo and professional contact details — are published in the exhibitor directory on this website. That is the purpose of the directory and the reason exhibitors register.

Data of participants registered as buyers, media or any profile other than exhibitor is never published on this website. Photographs of individuals submitted during registration are not published either: the directory shows companies, not people.

7. International transfers

This website loads content from services provided by companies based outside the European Economic Area, which involves an international transfer of the technical data any internet request carries, your IP address among it:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — supplies one of the typefaces. Loads with every page.
  • YouTube (i.ytimg.com) — supplies the still images of the two videos. The player itself is loaded only when you press play, and in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).
  • Issuu (e.issuu.com) — displays the destination brochures, and is loaded only when you open one.

The event platform and the newsletter service are likewise operated by third parties with their own infrastructure. In all cases, compliance with data protection law is ensured through the safeguards provided for in Chapter V of the GDPR.

Nothing else is contacted while you browse. There is no advertising network, no social media pixel and no analytics service on this site.

8. Your rights

  • Access to your personal data
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure, where among other reasons the data is no longer necessary
  • Restriction of processing
  • Objection to processing
  • Data portability
  • Not to be subject to automated decisions, where applicable

To exercise these rights, write to datuak@bilbao.eus, or to Bilbao Ekintza at the address above, stating which right you wish to exercise and proving your identity.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Basque Data Protection Authority — www.avpd.eus.

For data held on the event platform, requests may also be addressed to the organising team, who will pass them on to the platform provider.

9. Categories of data

  • Identification data (name and surname)
  • Contact data (email address, telephone)
  • Professional data (company, position, markets, interests)
  • Data relating to the enquiry or to participation in the event
  • Browsing data, limited to the server's technical access logs

10. Security

Bilbao Ekintza adopts the technical and organisational measures necessary to guarantee the security of personal data and to prevent its alteration, loss, unauthorised processing or unauthorised access, taking into account the state of the art, the nature of the data and the risks to which it is exposed.

11. Changes to this policy

Bilbao Ekintza reserves the right to amend this privacy policy to reflect changes in legislation, case law or sector practice, and to reflect changes in how this website works. Any change will be announced on this page, and the date of the last update is shown at the top.
