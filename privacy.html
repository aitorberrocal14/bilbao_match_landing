2. What this website collects: nothing

This site is made of static pages. It has no contact form, no search box that reaches a server, no login, no shopping basket and no analytics or audience measurement of any kind. It does not set cookies and it does not store anything in your browser.

As with any website, the server that delivers these pages may record technical access data — IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type — in its logs. Those logs are kept by the hosting provider for security and maintenance, and are not used to build any profile or to identify visitors.

3. When you write to us

If you send a message to welcome@matchbilbaobizkaia.eus or call the telephone number published on the site, we process the data you give us in order to answer you and to help you take part in the event.

  • Legal basis: your request, and our legitimate interest in answering it.
  • Retention: for as long as the exchange requires, and thereafter for the period during which any liability may arise.
  • Recipients: only the organising team, and the email provider that delivers the message.

4. When you subscribe to the newsletter

The newsletter buttons on this site do not collect your address here. They take you to a subscription form hosted by Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.), where the data is entered and your consent is recorded. That form and the subsequent mailings are governed by the privacy policy of the organisation that sends the newsletter and by Mailchimp's own terms.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every mailing.

5. When you register for the event

Registration, your profile, your availability and your meeting agenda are handled on the event platform operated by Meetmaps, a third-party service. The data you enter there is processed to organise the event: to accredit you, to match you with other participants and to schedule the meetings.

Exhibitors: if you register as an exhibitor, the business details of your company — company name, category, logo and professional contact details — are published in the exhibitor directory on this website, so that international buyers can find you. That is the purpose of the directory and the reason exhibitors register.

Data of participants registered as buyers, media or any profile other than exhibitor is never published on this website. Photographs of individuals submitted during registration are not published either: the directory shows companies, not people.

6. Third-party services used by the pages

Some elements of the site are served by third parties. When they load, your browser connects to them and, as with any internet request, they receive your IP address and basic technical data:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — supplies one of the typefaces. Loads with every page.
  • YouTube (i.ytimg.com) — supplies the still images of the two videos. The video player itself is only loaded when you press play, and it is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).
  • Issuu (e.issuu.com) — displays the destination brochures, and is only loaded when you open one.

Nothing else is contacted while you browse. In particular there is no advertising network, no social media pixel and no analytics service on this site.

7. Transfers outside the European Union

The third-party services named above are provided by companies based in the United States, which may involve an international transfer of data. Those providers operate under the transfer mechanisms set out in Chapter V of the GDPR. Their own privacy policies describe their processing in detail.
