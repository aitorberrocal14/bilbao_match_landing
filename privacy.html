In short: two different things happen under the name Match Bilbao Bizkaia, and they are worth telling apart. This website has no forms, no accounts and no analytics: it asks you for nothing and records nothing about you. The event platform, at meetmaps.com, is where you register, build your profile and arrange your meetings — and that is where personal data is actually processed. This page covers both, and says which is which throughout.

2. Two places, and what happens in each

Everything in this policy belongs to one of two places. Which one it is changes what is collected, who can see it and how long it is kept, so each section below says plainly which place it is talking about.

  • This website (matchbilbaobizkaia.eus) — the public pages: the programme, the destination, the exhibitor directory and the brochures. Nothing is collected here.
  • The event platform (meetmaps.com) — registration, your professional profile, your availability, the meeting requests and your agenda. This is where personal data is processed, and it is a different website with its own terms of use, reached by pressing Login or Register.

3. This website

It does not collect anything. These pages are static. There is no contact form, no subscription form, no login and no analytics or audience measurement of any kind. No cookies are set, nothing is written to your browser's local or session storage, and nothing is sent to us as you read.

The pages are also served without contacting anyone else in order to display themselves: the typefaces, the images and the styles all come from this site. Content from other services is loaded only if you ask for it — the video player when you press play, a brochure when you open one — and the cookie policy lists which ones and what that means.

As with any website, the server that delivers these pages keeps technical access logs — IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system — for the security and correct operation of the site. Those logs are not used to build any profile or to identify visitors.

The one thing this website publishes is the exhibitor directory: the business details of the companies taking part as exhibitors — company name, category, logo and professional contact details. That is the purpose of the directory and the reason exhibitors register. Those details reach this site from the platform. Participants registered as buyers, media or any other profile are never published here, and neither are photographs of individuals: the directory shows companies, not people.

4. The event platform: what your data is used for

When you register for Match Bilbao Bizkaia you create a profile on the event platform. The data you enter there is used for the following purposes, all of them part of running the event:

  • Registration and participation. Managing your registration, your profile as a participant and your taking part in the event.
  • Accreditation and attendance. Issuing your accreditation and managing access to the venues and sessions of the programme.
  • Meetings and professional activities. Organising the pre-scheduled B2B meetings, the workshop and the rest of the professional programme, including your availability and your agenda.
  • Networking and matching. Suggesting and enabling contact between participants whose profiles and interests fit, which is the purpose of the event.
  • Your professional profile. Building and keeping up to date the profile that describes your company, your markets and what you are looking for.
  • Visibility to other participants. Showing part of your profile to the other people registered for the event, so they can decide whom to ask to meet. Section 5 says which part.
  • Communications about the event. Writing to you about the organisation and running of the event: confirmations, changes to the programme, practical information and your meeting schedule.

If data is ever to be used for a different purpose, the person concerned will be informed beforehand.
