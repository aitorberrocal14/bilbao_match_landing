Cookie policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This website does not use cookies. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for preferences. That is why you have not been asked to accept anything: there is nothing to consent to.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file that a website asks your browser to store, so that it can recognise your browser on a later visit. Cookies are used to keep you signed in, to remember preferences, to measure audiences and to target advertising.

Under Article 22.2 of Spanish Act 34/2002 (LSSI-CE), a website must obtain your consent before storing information on your device, except where it is strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested. This site stores nothing at all, so no consent is required and no banner is shown.

2. What this site actually does

The pages are static. They set no cookies, and they write nothing to your browser's local storage or session storage. Nothing about your visit is kept on your device, so nothing identifies you when you come back.

There is no analytics or audience measurement service on this site. We do not know how many people visit it, which pages they read or where they come from, beyond the technical access logs that the hosting provider keeps for security and maintenance.

3. Content embedded from third parties

Three services outside this site are used to display content. When they load, your browser connects to them, and they receive your IP address and basic technical data — as happens with any internet request. None of them places cookies on this site under normal browsing:

ServiceWhat it is forWhen it loads
Google Fonts One of the two typefaces used on the site With every page
YouTube The still image of any video that does not carry its own With the pages that show them
YouTube (no-cookie mode) The video player itself Only when you press play
Issuu The destination brochures Only when you open a brochure

The video player is loaded through youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which does not record what you watch unless you play the video. Neither the player nor the brochure viewer is loaded until you ask for it, so simply reading the page contacts neither.

4. Other websites are a separate matter

Registration and sign-in happen on the event platform, which is a different website operated by a third party. It does use cookies — a service with accounts and sessions cannot work without them — and it has its own cookie policy, which you will be shown when you go there.

The same applies to the other sites linked from here, including bilbaoekintza.eus and the institutional websites of the destination. Each has its own cookie policy, and what is said on this page is true of this website only.

5. How to control cookies in general

There is nothing to disable on this site, but every browser lets you review and delete the cookies stored by the sites you visit, and block them in advance. The setting is usually under Privacy or Privacy and security, in the browser's own settings.

6. Changes to this policy

If this site ever adds a tool that stores information on your device, this page will be rewritten and a consent request will be added before that tool is loaded. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
